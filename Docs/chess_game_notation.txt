chess_game_notation.txt	03Sep2026

Used all over the world
to record games
in tournament play
in text transmission
and storage

Most common and recognized internationally is
called algebraic notation

Example
1. e4 e5
2. f4 exf4
3. Bc4 Qh4+
...Ommitted for brevity
4. 0-1

We'll skip the move numbers
and separation.

Let's look at the move
specification string.
Example: Bc4 (Bishop moves
				to square c4)

The primary goal of the
move specification is to
specify:
1. the moving piece
(king, queen, rook,
bishop, knight, or pawn)

2. the destination square
file and row
[ordering is from the
white player's persepctive]

column: known as file
by letter, left to right, a-h

row: known as rank by numbers,
bottom to top 1-8

3. Other move attributes
such as check, checkmate,
game result
